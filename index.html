A community reputation

Rate the people and places you actually meet.

Meetyr is an iPhone app for building trust in your community. Discover people and businesses near you, give a quick thumbs up or thumbs down, and grow your own Meetyr score from every real interaction.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

Everyone on Meetyr has a real profile with a photo, and every score comes from real encounters.

◎

Discover nearby

See people and businesses within the distance you choose, from one mile to anywhere.

👍

Rate in a tap

Thumbs up or thumbs down. Ratings add up to a score that reflects how a community sees you.

▣

Scan to rate

Met someone in person? Scan their Meetyr QR code to rate them directly.

Built for safety

A reputation app only works if people feel safe using it.

Real profiles

Every account confirms its email and adds a photo, name, category and short bio before it can rate.

Report and block

Report harassment or fake accounts from any profile. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.

You stay in control

Hide your bio, make your profile private, pause your account, or delete it at any time.
