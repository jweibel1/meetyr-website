For business owners

Claim your business, get verified, or ask us for a change.

Found your business on Meetyr without signing up? Meetyr lists some local businesses from open map data so members can find and rate them, marked "Not on Meetyr yet". These listings aren't connected to Meetyr. You can claim yours, ask us to fix it, or ask us to remove it. Each listing shows its street address, so you can be sure you're claiming your own location, which matters if your brand has several.

Claim your listing

In the app, open your business's profile and tap Own this business? Claim it or ask for a change, or email support@meetyr.com with your business name and address. We check two things, usually within 2 business days:

  • Your business is real: its website, a Google or Apple Maps listing, or a state business registration shows the same name and address.
  • You speak for it: you email us from an address at your business's own website, we call the phone number your business shows publicly, or we send you a code to post on your website or social page.

Then the listing's account is yours, with a Verified badge. When you claim it, you choose once: keep its ratings, or start fresh with a new account, which hides all of the listing's ratings. Either way, you'll see who rated you from then on, but never how anyone rated you.

Already on Meetyr? Get verified

Every business starts unverified. Go to Me → Edit profile → Get verified in the app. Verified businesses get a green check. "Verified" means we checked your business is real and that you speak for it. It isn't an endorsement.

Ratings are members' own

  • Ratings are a thumbs up or down about a member's visit, and a member can rate your business once a day. There are no written reviews.
  • Businesses can't erase or remove ratings. If you think a rating is fake, flag it from your Feed: a moderator decides on whether the visit was real, never on whether the rating was good or bad.
  • Please don't offer anything in exchange for ratings, and don't rate your own business.
  • If your business has moved or closed, email support@meetyr.com with the new address, a contact name and a phone number. Once your business is verified, our team checks and makes address changes; they can't be made in the app.

See who's here

Members can choose to let businesses on Meetyr know when they're there. Once your business is verified, your Feed shows those members' names and photos under Here now once they've been there a few minutes and their phone clearly puts them at your business, and for 12 hours after. Where businesses are close together, such as in a food court or a mall, a member shows up once they rate your business after their visit. Only members who turned this on appear, and you never see how anyone rated you.

Ask for a change or removal

Email support@meetyr.com. We fix wrong details, hide businesses that have closed, and remove listings that are someone's home or a person rather than a business. We don't remove a listing because of its ratings. Where the listing details come from: Data credits.
