Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Meetyr LLC ("Meetyr", "we", "us") collects when you use the Meetyr app and this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email support@meetyr.com.

The short version: we collect what's needed to run a community rating app: your account, your profile, your location while you use the app (and, only if you turn on visit features, the businesses you visit), and the ratings you give and receive. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. The iPhone and Android apps contain no analytics or advertising tools; our website and the web version of Meetyr count visits without cookies.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form.
  • Profile: your display name, @handle, profile photo, whether you're a person or a business, a category, and a short "About" text.
  • Ratings: the thumbs-up or thumbs-down ratings you give, and when you gave them.
  • Rating requests: when you rate a person more than 1 mile away, your confirmation that you're rating your interaction with them, and their answer.
  • Private profile: whether your profile is private, and, for each rating you give, whether it was private when you gave it.
  • Safety reports: when you report a member or flag a rating you received, the reason you choose and any details you write. We also keep a record of members you block.
  • Business verification and claims: when a business asks to be verified, claims a listing, or changes its address, the details it sends us (such as its website, a contact name and phone number, the person's role and how we can confirm them) and our record of when and how we checked. We don't collect ID documents.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to our support address.

Information collected when you use the app

  • Location: with your permission, your device's location while the app is open. We use it to show you people and businesses nearby, to show others how far away you are, and to place you on the Discover map (see below). We store your most recent location, not a history of where you've been. The app uses location in the background only if you turn on visit features (see below).
  • Home area: when you set up a personal profile, we ask where you're located (a ZIP code, or your current location). We keep only a rough area about a mile across and its town and ZIP code, never your address. While your distance is hidden, Discover shows you to people near this area instead of near where you are. You can change it in Edit profile.
  • Ratings you receive: your score and the number of ratings you've received.
  • QR code: a random code linked to your profile so others can scan it in person to rate you. Each code works for one scan; you get a new one after every scan. We keep a record of a scan only for the 10 minutes the person scanning has to rate you.
  • Camera: used only when you take a profile photo or scan a QR code. Camera images aren't stored except the profile photo you choose.
  • Notifications: only if you turn them on. Visit reminders, and a "Remind me tomorrow" after you scan a business's code, are set up by your phone itself. Group notifications are push notifications we send (iPhone only): if you turn them on in Me → Group notifications, we save your phone's push token with your account. They tell you who invited you to a private group, that there's a new question in a group (with the group's name), and when your question closes, never a question or an answer. A verified business that turns on push also gets notices about its customer group. Turning them off, signing out or deleting your account removes the token.

We don't use advertising identifiers or tracking across other companies' apps and websites. meetyr.com and the web version of Meetyr count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, doesn't identify you, and doesn't follow you to other websites. The iPhone and Android apps don't use it.

Visit reminders and "Let businesses know when I'm there"

Both are off unless you turn them on in Me → Visits, and you never need either one to rate. Business accounts don't have them.

  • How a visit is noticed: if you allow location "Always", your phone watches the 19 businesses nearest you and notices when you stay at one. Your phone does the watching. When you've moved about a mile, it asks us for the businesses near its new spot; we use that location only to answer and don't store it. With location "While using", the app checks only when you open it at a business.
  • Visit notes: when you visit one of those businesses, we keep a note of which business and when you arrived and left, never your exact location. Notes are deleted after 24 hours, and turning visit reminders off erases yours. A note lets you rate a listed business for 12 hours after you leave.
  • Reminders: after a visit of at least 5 minutes, one notification asks how it was. At most one per business a day, and none once you've rated it. While "Rate from the reminder" is on (it starts on with visit reminders), a reminder about one business names it, on the lock screen too, and lets you rate it there. Turn it off in Me → Visits and reminders never name the place. A "Remind me tomorrow" names the business too.
  • Letting businesses know: if you turn this on, a verified business on Meetyr sees your name and photo once you've been there a few minutes, if your phone's location clearly puts you at that business, and for 12 hours after. Where businesses are close together, only the one you rate after your visit sees you. It never sees your thumb or where else you go, but a rating you make while it can see you may give your thumb away, since scores move within minutes. A verified business can also turn on arrival alerts, a notification that "A Meetyr member is here now", which never names you. Turning this off hides you from businesses right away.
  • Places we never watch: sensitive places, such as health care, places of worship, bars, dispensaries, pawn shops, lawyers and adult stores. We never sell visit information or use it for ads.

What other members can see

  • Your display name, @handle, profile photo, person or business, category, Meetyr score and number of ratings.
  • Your "About" text, unless you hide it in Edit profile.
  • How far away you are (for example, "0.4 mi"), unless you turn this off in Edit profile.
  • Where you are on the Discover map. People appear only as a rough area (about a quarter mile across), never their exact spot or address. Turning off your distance in Edit profile also takes you off the map.
  • Business accounts are always public. A business's About, distance and business location are shown to everyone. A business sets its location by entering an address or using its current location; it doesn't follow the owner's phone. Businesses that go to their customers instead appear as a rough area, like people.
  • That you rated them. When you rate someone, they see your name in their Feed, but never whether you gave a thumbs up or a thumbs down. Names show 3 at a time. Scores move within about 15 minutes of each rating, so someone watching their score may be able to tell how a person they know just rated them, for example after a QR scan or a request they confirmed. A private profile shows "A private member" instead of your name. A person's score shows once ratings from 3 people count in it; a business's from its first rating.
  • Private profiles. A person can make their profile private in Edit profile. Whoever they rate then sees "A private member" instead of their name, photo and profile, and ratings made while private stay that way even after it's turned off. A private profile is still shown in Discover and Search and can still be rated. Private members can't send rating requests (they rate people within 1 mile or by QR code), turning it on withdraws any requests still waiting, and businesses never see them in "Here now". We can still see who made every rating, and whether it was up or down, to keep Meetyr safe.
  • Erasers. Each person (not business) has one eraser. It takes every rating they received in the last 7 days out of their score at once. We keep an erased rating on file, marked as erased. Profiles of people say that a score can leave out a week of erased ratings.
  • Requests to rate beyond a mile. If you rate a person more than 1 mile away, they're asked to confirm they had an interaction with you before the rating counts. They see your name, never your rating. Businesses don't confirm ratings.
  • Legends. Legends are historical figures and storybook characters you can rate for fun. They aren't members and can't rate anyone. When your rating of a Legend counts, your name, @handle and photo appear in its "Rated by" list, which other members can see, but never your thumb. You aren't listed if your profile was private when you rated or is private now, and members you've blocked, or who blocked you, don't see you there. A Legend's score moves right away, so someone watching it may be able to tell how you rated it. We keep each Legend rating with your account (your thumb, the question and whether your answer was right) and delete it if you delete your account.
  • That you're visiting a business, only if you turn on "Let businesses know when I'm there" (see above).

The app never shows anyone how a particular member rated them.

Businesses Meetyr lists from open data

So members can find and rate local businesses before they join, Meetyr lists some businesses itself, marked "Not on Meetyr yet". Their names, kinds of business, locations and street addresses come from open map data published by the Overture Maps Foundation, which combines data from Meta, Microsoft, Foursquare and others (see Data credits). These listings aren't connected to Meetyr or endorsed by the businesses.

  • We list storefront businesses only. We leave out homes and businesses run from home or that travel to their customers, health care, places of worship, schools and childcare, government offices, and listings that look like one person's practice.
  • We don't import phone numbers, email addresses, websites or social media accounts. A listing's street address is shown on its profile, so an owner can be sure which location is theirs.
  • Members can rate a listed business within 1 mile of it, or for 12 hours after a visit noticed with visit reminders. The first rating gives the listing an account that nobody can sign in to until its owner claims it.
  • If a business claims its listing, it sees who rated it, including before it joined (private members show as "A private member"), but never anyone's thumb.
  • To ask us to fix or remove a listing, tap the shield on its profile or email support@meetyr.com. We remove listings quickly if they're someone's home or a person rather than a business. We don't remove a listing because of its ratings.

How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, and to sign you in.
  • To show nearby profiles, calculate scores and display ratings.
  • To send account emails, such as confirmation and password reset codes.
  • To remind you to rate a business you visited and, if you choose, let it know you're there.
  • To count, in totals only, how many members sign up, are active and rate, so we can run and improve Meetyr.
  • To keep Meetyr safe: reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms of Use and preventing abuse or fraud.
  • To respond when you contact us, and to meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that run Meetyr for us under contract: Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage), Resend (sending account emails, and alerting our team when a report comes in), Cloudflare (counting visits to our website and the web version, without cookies), and Expo (delivering app updates; it receives a random install number, never your name or account, and an error message if an update fails to start; and, if you turn on notifications we send, passing them to Apple to deliver: it receives your phone's push token and each notification's text), and Apple (delivering notifications to your iPhone). They may only use your information to provide their services to us.
  • Address lookup: when a business enters its address, or you enter your ZIP code, it is looked up by Apple's maps service in the iPhone app, or by OpenStreetMap on the web, to find where it is on the map.
  • Other members, limited to the profile information described above.
  • Authorities, when required by law, or when needed to protect someone's safety or the rights of Meetyr and its members.
  • A buyer or successor, if Meetyr LLC is ever involved in a merger or sale, under this same policy.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app under Me → Edit profile.
  • Hide your About text or your distance in Edit profile (not available for business accounts, which are always public). Hiding your distance also takes you off the Discover map.
  • Location: turn location access off in your iPhone's Settings. Nearby features need it to work.
  • Visits: turn visit reminders and "Let businesses know when I'm there" on or off under Me → Visits. Turning visit reminders off erases your visit notes. You can also change Meetyr's location access from "Always" to "While using" in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Pause your account under Me → Safety & reporting → Suspend my account.
  • Delete your account in the app under Me → Safety & reporting → Delete my account (or from the paused screen if you've suspended it). You can also email support@meetyr.com from the address you signed up with.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photo, location, QR code, visit notes, and the ratings you gave and received. A rating you gave stops counting toward another member's score at the first score update after midnight Pacific time, not at the moment you leave. Until then only the number remains, without your name. Copies may remain in our secure backups for up to 90 days before they're removed. We may keep limited records longer when the law requires it or to resolve a safety report. Visit notes are deleted after 24 hours.

If you delete your account, we keep any reports made about you, with your name, @handle and the date you deleted it, for 90 days. Only Meetyr's team can see them, so someone can't escape a report by deleting their account. After 90 days they're deleted.

If a business that claimed its listing deletes its account, the listing stays on Meetyr with its ratings, and support can hand it to a new owner.

When a listed business is closed, removed, or replaced because its owner chose to start fresh, we hide it and keep its ratings on file, not shown and not counted in any score, for moderation and legal records.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls that let each member reach only their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

Age requirement

Meetyr is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, other U.S. states or the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, and to object to certain uses. Email support@meetyr.com to make a request. We'll respond within the time the law requires and won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app or by email.
