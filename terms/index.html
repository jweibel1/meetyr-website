4. Ratings and scores

Ratings are the personal opinions of members about their most recent interaction with a person, or a visit to a business, not statements of fact by Meetyr and never a judgment of anyone's looks. We don't verify individual ratings and we aren't responsible for them, but we remove ratings that break these terms. Scores are calculated automatically from ratings and may change over time. New ratings join a score in groups of at least five (checked each night at midnight Pacific time), or once a week when ratings from at least two people are waiting. Ratings that are removed (erased, taken down by us, or from a deleted account) stop counting only together with at least five new ratings.

A rating of a person more than 1 mile away counts only after they confirm they had an interaction with you. Businesses don't confirm ratings, and a member can rate a business once a day (Pacific time). Each QR code works for one scan. Members can see who rated them in their Feed, but never whether a rating was a thumbs up or down; a rating from a member whose profile was private when they made it, or is private now, shows as "A private member". Private members can't send rating requests, and we can always see who made a rating.

Each person has one eraser: it removes one rating they received, chosen without seeing whether it was a thumbs up or down, so a rating you give may be erased. Business accounts have no eraser. If you're rated for professional services you offer (for example as a realtor, trainer or contractor), use a business account.

5. Businesses and listings

  • Verification. Every business starts unverified. "Verified" means Meetyr checked that the business is real at the place on its profile and that the person running the account speaks for it. It isn't an endorsement. Verified businesses show a green check and can rate people; a business that isn't verified can be rated but can't rate anyone. We may remove a badge if a business closes, moves without telling us, or isn't what it said.
  • Changes to a verified business. A verified business changes its address, name and category through support@meetyr.com. We check a new address, and who is asking, before the business's pin moves.
  • Listings. Meetyr lists some businesses that haven't joined, from open map data, marked "Not on Meetyr yet". Listing details are shown as they are and may be out of date; members can report problems. Listed businesses can be rated within 1 mile, or for 12 hours after a visit noticed with visit reminders.
  • Claiming a listing. An owner who passes verification takes over the listing's account. When claiming, they choose once to keep its ratings or start fresh with a new account, which hides all of the listing's ratings (we keep them on file). Nobody can have a listing or its ratings removed because they dislike them.
  • No erasers for businesses. Business accounts can't erase ratings.
